About us

Trucking run by people who actually truck.

Dijla Trucking is a small, owner-operated reefer carrier based in Boise, Idaho. We drive too, so we understand the job from the driver's seat, not a corporate office.

We keep things simple: dependable equipment, clear communication, realistic expectations, and respect for the driver doing the work.

We're looking for an experienced, safety-minded driver who wants steady OTR work with a company small enough to know their name.
